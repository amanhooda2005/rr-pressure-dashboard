Method in brief

Pressure. A win-probability model (gradient boosting, validated out-of-fold by match; chase AUC ) is fitted to every ball. Each ball's Pressure Index is the expected swing in win probability on the next ball, relative to an average ball, ×1.2 in knockouts. The top 25% of balls are "high pressure".

Performance. Every ball's change in win probability is credited to the striker, the dismissed batter and the bowler. It is divided by the Pressure Index and compared with the average for the same innings × phase × pressure level (and pace vs spin for bowlers).

Score. PRS = 0.50·Pressure impact + 0.25·Consistency (share of pressure innings or spells that were net-positive) + 0.25·Lift (pressure impact minus normal impact), as z-scores. Small samples are shrunk toward the average; recent seasons and stronger opposition carry more weight; eligibility needs ≥120 pressure balls faced or ≥150 bowled, and a game in 2025 or 2026.

Robustness. Final order = average of main-model rank, mean rank across 12 alternative set-ups and median rank across 300 match reshuffles. Read the top 10 as tiers: pressure performance repeats only weakly from match to match.
